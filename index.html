ABOUT

Minecraft.nat is a Minecraft-style clone: a blocky sandbox where you explore, mine and build. It's just a fun little side project, made for learning and for the love of making games, with no big ambitions attached.

It's a work in progress, so expect bugs, odd corners and features that appear when inspiration strikes.
