About Minecraft.net

This is not the official Minecraft website. It’s a tiny Minecraft‑style clone I’m building as a fun little sideproject—something to experiment with game mechanics, rendering tricks, and blocky worlds without worrying about perfection.

The goal isn’t to compete with the real game. It’s to learn, play, and ship something cool: a sandbox where you can place blocks, explore a small world, and maybe break things in the process (in a good way).

Expect bugs. Expect weird physics. Expect features to appear and disappear as ideas evolve. If you enjoy experimental projects and indie vibes, you’re in the right place.

Project Vibes

• Blocky worlds, simple controls • Lightweight build you can run quickly • A playground for new ideas, not a polished AAA release • Perfect for people who love tinkering and seeing how games are made

Want to peek under the hood? The project is hosted on GitHub, so you can explore the code, fork it, or even contribute your own experiments.
